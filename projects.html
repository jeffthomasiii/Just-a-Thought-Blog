Selected Projects

Some thoughts become essays. Others become things I build.

❦

Just A Thought is primarily a place where I process faith, life, relationships, leadership, culture, and technology through writing. Sometimes, however, an idea begins to take the shape of something more practical: a prototype, a digital tool, or an experiment designed to solve a real problem.

These are selected projects that have developed far enough to share, use, and continue refining. They are not finished commercial products, but they represent another part of how I think: noticing a need, asking better questions, and building something that may help.

BlueGreen Guide

Discover better outdoors.

BlueGreen Guide is an outdoor discovery and wayfinding project designed to help people find practical experiences across blue spaces and green spaces.

BlueGreen Guide is currently in alpha testing with a deliberately curated set of outdoor locations across Southern and Central California, Arizona, and Nevada. The current test dataset is intentionally limited while I evaluate the experience in real-world use, gather feedback, and refine the wayfinding, search, map, and location information before expanding into additional areas and regions.

The larger idea is simple: make it easier for people to understand where they can go, what they can do there, and what they should know before heading outdoors.

  • Interactive outdoor discovery map
  • Search, filters, and curated collections
  • Public documentation and field guidance

PawPath

Pet-care preparedness for the road.

PawPath is a mobile-friendly project for campers, RV travelers, road-trippers, and others who travel with pets.

Instead of waiting until something goes wrong, PawPath helps travelers identify nearby veterinary-care options, choose a primary and backup facility, and save essential trip and pet information before leaving home. It also includes a separate experience for finding nearby care when it is needed more urgently.

The project grew from a practical question: when you are traveling with a pet, how prepared are you to find appropriate care in an unfamiliar place?

  • Trip planning and immediate-care search modes
  • Primary and backup veterinary-care selections
  • Locally saved care plans without an account

Why These Belong Here

Building can be another form of reflection.

These projects are not separate from the ideas explored throughout Just A Thought. BlueGreen Guide grows from a love of creation, camping, and practical discovery. PawPath grows from traveling with pets and thinking ahead about the ordinary responsibilities that come with caring for them well.

Writing helps me understand an idea. Building sometimes helps me test whether that idea can become useful to someone else.

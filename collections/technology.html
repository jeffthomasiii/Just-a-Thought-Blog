It changes how we work, communicate, learn, create, lead, and sometimes even how we understand what people are for. That makes new technology worth approaching with both curiosity and restraint rather than either fear or unquestioned enthusiasm. This collection gathers reflections on artificial intelligence, digital life, ministry, design technology, ethics, innovation, stewardship, work, and human flourishing. The goal is not to predict every technological turn. It is to keep asking what wisdom, responsibility, and faithful practice look like as the tools around us change.

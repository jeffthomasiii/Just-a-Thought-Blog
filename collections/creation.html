Others show up beside a lake, on a long drive, around a campsite, or somewhere between the pines and the shoreline. Getting outdoors does not automatically make a moment spiritual, but slowing down often gives us room to notice what speed and routine can hide. This collection gathers reflections shaped by camping, travel, nature, rest, stillness, adventure, marriage connection, landscapes, and the rhythms of the created world. It is less about outdoor expertise than about the perspective that can come when familiar distractions grow quieter.

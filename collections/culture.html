It is the collection of ideas, assumptions, habits, words, stories, and expectations that quietly teach us what is normal, desirable, frightening, admirable, or true. Paying attention to culture therefore requires more than reacting to whatever is loudest today. This collection gathers reflections on discernment, language, media, social patterns, public life, Christian thinking, fear, truth, and the challenge of engaging the world faithfully without simply consuming or condemning it. The aim is thoughtful attention rather than outrage.

--- layout: collection title: "Faith" subtitle: "Scripture, discipleship, formation, and the daily walk with God" description: "A curated collection of reflections on Scripture, Christian discipleship, spiritual formation, hope, wisdom, obedience, and learning to follow Christ in ordinary life." permalink: /collections/faith/ collection_key: faith icon: "✝" background: /img/posts/bg-created-fallen-restored.jpg intro_heading: "Faith is more than a subject to study." start_here: - "Created, Fallen, Restored" - "The Difference Hope Makes" - "Turning the Page" - "The War Within" --- Faith shows up throughout *Just A Thought* because it shapes the way I process nearly everything else. Some reflections begin directly in Scripture. Others begin with a question, a conversation, an ordinary experience, or something happening in the culture around us and eventually lead me back to what I believe about God, people, and the life we are called to live. This collection gathers those reflections into one place. It is not meant to function like a systematic theology or a step-by-step discipleship course. Think of it instead as a shelf of connected thoughts about Scripture, obedience, spiritual formation, suffering, hope, wisdom, identity, and what it can look like to follow Christ faithfully in everyday life. You can begin with the curated reflections below, follow one of the longer series, start with the newest additions, or simply browse the full collection until something meets the question you are carrying today.

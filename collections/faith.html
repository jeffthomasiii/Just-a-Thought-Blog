Some reflections begin directly in Scripture. Others begin with a question, a conversation, an ordinary experience, or something happening in the culture around us and eventually lead me back to what I believe about God, people, and the life we are called to live. This collection gathers those reflections into one place. It is not meant to function like a systematic theology or a step-by-step discipleship course.
